Project Overview

The City of Wapakoneta's Administration Building was experiencing severe roof leaks. Nitro Roofing inspected the metal roof, traced the leaks to improperly installed fasteners and failing trim components, and completed a targeted commercial roof repair that left the roof fully sealed.

The Challenge

Severe Roof Leaks at City Administration Building

The City of Wapakoneta's Administration Building began experiencing severe roof leaks that threatened the integrity of the building and its operations. After an inspection, the Nitro Roofing team discovered that improperly installed fasteners and failing trim components were allowing water to penetrate the roofing system.

Over time, these faulty fasteners created multiple entry points for water, leading to ongoing leaks and the potential for costly structural damage if not corrected quickly.

Nitro Roofing's Solution

Nitro Roofing performed a targeted commercial roof repair to eliminate the source of the leaks.

Our team removed all problematic fasteners and trim components that were contributing to water intrusion. These were then replaced with new, properly installed fasteners and updated trim materials designed to create a durable, watertight seal.

This repair restored the integrity of the roof system while preventing further moisture penetration.

The Result

Leak-Free Roof and Protected Municipal Building

After the repair was completed, the Administration Building's roof was fully sealed and protected from further leaks.

By correcting the faulty installation and replacing the compromised components, Nitro Roofing was able to restore the building's roofing system and prevent further damage, ensuring the city's administrative offices remain protected for years to come.
