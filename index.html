Working With Us

Our Simple 6 Step Process

  1. 01

    Step 1: Initial Consultation

    Call us and speak with our customer service team. We'll answer your questions and schedule a convenient time for our local Nitro representative to visit your property.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Professional Inspection & Estimate

    Our experienced Nitro representative conducts a thorough roof inspection and provides you with a detailed, no-obligation estimate.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Project Approval & Scheduling

    Once you approve the estimate, we enter your project into our system and schedule your roof replacement (typically within 6-10 weeks).

  4. 04

    Step 4: Pre-Project Communication

    Our production team contacts you before your scheduled start date via call, email, and/or text to confirm details and answer any final questions.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Expert Installation

    Our on-site supervisor oversees the entire 1-2 day roofing project, ensuring quality workmanship. We handle complete cleanup—nothing gets left behind.

  6. 06

    Step 6: Final Walkthrough & Completion

    Your original Nitro representative conducts a final walkthrough with you to ensure everything meets our high standards, then we handle billing and request your feedback.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential roof replacements are completed in 1-2 days, depending on the size and complexity of your roof. Weather conditions and unforeseen issues can occasionally extend the timeline, but we keep you informed every step of the way.

How much does a roof replacement cost?

Every roof is different, which is why we provide free inspections and detailed estimates tailored to your specific needs. Factors like roof size, pitch, materials, and complexity all affect pricing. Contact us for an accurate, no-obligation estimate.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Yes. We have extensive experience working with insurance companies on storm damage claims. We'll help you navigate the process, document the damage, and work to get you a fair settlement.

What's included in the warranty?

Your roof replacement includes both manufacturer warranties on materials and our workmanship warranty covering installation. We'll review all warranty details with you during the final walkthrough so you know exactly what's covered.

What happens if there's an issue after installation?

We stand behind our work. If you experience any issues related to our installation, contact us and we'll make it right. That's our commitment to you.

What type of siding is best for Ohio homes?

Vinyl siding is the most popular choice due to its durability, low maintenance, and affordability. Fiber cement is ideal for homeowners seeking premium performance and appearance. We'll help you choose the best option for your needs and budget.
