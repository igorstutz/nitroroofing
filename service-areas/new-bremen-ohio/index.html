Roofing services

Comprehensive Roofing Services for New Bremen & New Knoxville Homeowners

We provide complete residential roofing and exterior solutions designed to protect your home from Ohio's unpredictable weather and enhance your property's value and curb appeal.

Roof Replacement

Your roof is your home's first line of defense. When it's time for a replacement, our experienced team installs premium roofing systems using quality materials from industry-leading manufacturers like GAF. We handle every detail, from initial inspection and material selection to professional installation and thorough cleanup, ensuring your new roof protects your family for decades to come.

Signs you need roof replacement:

  • Roof age exceeding 20-25 years
  • Missing, cracked, or curling shingles
  • Persistent leaks or water damage
  • Excessive granule loss in gutters
  • Rising energy bills due to poor insulation

Roof Repair

Not every roofing problem requires a full replacement. Our expert repair services fix leaks, replace damaged shingles, repair flashing, and address storm damage quickly and effectively. We provide honest assessments. If a repair will solve your problem and extend your roof's life, we'll tell you. We're here to provide cost-effective solutions, not unnecessary replacements.

Common repairs we handle:

  • Leak detection and repair
  • Shingle replacement
  • Flashing repairs around chimneys and vents
  • Storm damage restoration
  • Emergency repairs

Our process

Our Simple Roofing Process

We've streamlined our approach to make roofing projects clear, efficient, and stress-free for homeowners.

Step 1:

Initial Consultation

Contact our customer service team. We'll answer your questions and schedule a convenient time for our Nitro representative to visit your property.

Step 2:

Professional Inspection & Estimate

Our experienced Nitro representative conducts a complete roof evaluation and provides you with a detailed, no-obligation estimate within days.

Step 3:

Project Approval & Scheduling

Once you approve the estimate, we enter your project into our system and schedule your work, typically within 6–10 weeks.

Step 4:

Pre-Project Communication

Our production team contacts you about 2 weeks before your scheduled start date via phone, email, or text to confirm details and answer any final questions.

Step 5:

Expert Installation

Our on-site supervisor oversees every aspect of the project, ensuring quality workmanship throughout. Most roofing projects are completed in 1 to 2 days. We handle complete cleanup — nothing gets left behind.

Step 6:

Final Walkthrough & Completion

Your Nitro representative conducts a final walkthrough with you to ensure everything meets our high standards, then we handle billing and request your feedback.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential roof replacements are completed in 1-2 days, depending on the size and complexity of your roof.
