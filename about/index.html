Who We Are

Protecting Ohio Homes With Honest Service & Quality Craftsmanship

Since 1999, Nitro Roofing & Construction has been protecting Ohio homes with quality craftsmanship, honest service, and a commitment to doing the job right. What began as a family-owned business has grown into a multigenerational company rooted in trust, accountability, and pride in every project we take on.

That legacy grew even stronger with the addition of Campbell's Roofing—a respected name with deep roots in the Ohio roofing industry. Together, we carry forward a combined legacy of over 100 years, built on generations of hands-on experience and a shared standard for doing things the right way.

At Nitro, we believe your home is more than just a structure—it's where your family's life unfolds and your legacy is built. That's why we approach every roof with the same level of care, precision, and responsibility we would expect for our own homes.

We're not just roofers. We're your neighbors. We're partners in protecting what matters most. And we're a team committed to being here for you—not just for this project, but for years to come.

Our Story

Founded in 1999

Nitro Roofing & Construction was founded in 1999 with a simple mission: to provide Ohio homeowners with honest roofing services they could trust. What began as a small operation built on hard work and integrity has grown into a multigenerational business that serves families throughout Columbus, New Bremen, Dayton, and surrounding communities.

Over the past 25+ years, we've seen the roofing industry change, but our commitment to quality craftsmanship and reliable service has never wavered. We've built our reputation one roof at a time, one satisfied customer at a time, and one referral at a time. That's how legacies are built.

In 2025, Nitro joined forces with Campbell's Roofing, bringing a combined business legacy of 100+ years to the community.

Today, Nitro is more than a roofing company. We're a team of dedicated professionals who understand that every roof we install protects a family, shelters memories, and secures a home for generations to come. We take that responsibility seriously.

From our first project in 1999 to the thousands of roofs we've installed since, our focus remains the same: doing the job right, standing behind our work, and being here when our customers need us. That's the Nitro way, and it's how we've earned the trust of Ohio homeowners for over 25 years.

Our mission is simple

Protecting What Matters To You

We believe that quality roofing isn't just about shingles and nails. It's about giving you peace of mind knowing your home is protected from Ohio's unpredictable weather, that your family is safe and dry, and that the roof over your head will stand strong for decades to come.

What drives us is the opportunity to serve our neighbors, solve real problems, and build lasting relationships with the families we protect. Whether it's repairing a leak before it causes major damage, helping you navigate a stressful insurance claim, or installing a brand-new roof that adds value and security to your home—we're here to make it right.

We're driven by craftsmanship, integrity, and a commitment to doing honest work. We don't cut corners. We don't walk away from our promises. And we don't just install roofs, we build trust, one project at a time.

What Sets Us Apart

Our Approach

In an industry where fly-by-night contractors come and go, Nitro Roofing & Construction has been here since 1999—backed by 100+ years of combined experience—and we're not going anywhere. But longevity is just one part of what makes us different. Here's what truly sets us apart:

At Nitro, we don't just meet industry standards, we set them. That's the difference 25+ years of experience, integrity, and commitment makes.

01.

On-Site Supervision

Most roofing companies don't provide on-site supervisors. We do. Every project is overseen by an experienced supervisor who ensures quality workmanship, addresses any issues immediately, and keeps your project on track from start to finish.

02.

Thorough Cleanup

We don't leave a mess behind. Our team conducts complete cleanup after every job—no nails, no debris, nothing left behind. Your property is treated with the same care and respect we'd give our own homes.

03.

Warranty-Backed Work

We stand behind every roof we install with comprehensive warranty protection. Our warranties aren't just paperwork—they're a promise that we'll be here when you need us, today and years from now.

04.

Transparent Process

From your first call to the final walkthrough, we keep you informed every step of the way. No surprises, no hidden fees, no runarounds. Just honest communication and straightforward service.

05.

Insurance Claim Expertise

Dealing with storm damage and insurance claims can be overwhelming. We handle the process for you, working directly with insurance companies to ensure you get a fair settlement and your roof gets restored properly. Learn about insurance restoration.

06.

Local Commitment

We're not a national chain. We're your Ohio neighbors, serving families throughout Columbus, New Bremen, Dayton, and surrounding areas. When you call, you get real people who care about your home and your experience.
