Signs

When You Need A Roof Replacement

Knowing when to replace your roof can save you from costly damage and give you peace of mind. Here are the signs it's time for a new roof:

01

Age of Your Roof

Most asphalt shingle roofs last 20-25 years. If your roof is approaching or past this age, it's time to consider a replacement before problems arise.

02

Visible Damage

Missing, cracked, or curling shingles are clear indicators your roof is failing. Storm damage, hail impact, and wear from weather exposure compromise your roof's ability to protect your home.

03

Persistent Leaks

If you're dealing with recurring leaks or water stains on your ceilings and walls, your roof may be beyond repair. Leaks lead to mold, structural damage, and costly interior repairs if left unaddressed.

04

Granule Loss

Check your gutters. If you're finding excessive granules (the gritty coating on shingles), your shingles are deteriorating and losing their protective layer.

05

Sagging or Structural Issues

A sagging roofline indicates serious structural problems that require immediate attention. This is a safety concern and needs professional evaluation right away.

06

Rising Energy Bills

Poor roof ventilation or failing insulation due to an aging roof can cause your heating and cooling costs to spike.

Delaying a necessary roof replacement only leads to bigger problems and higher costs. A new roof protects your family, preserves your home's value, and gives you the confidence that your investment is secure.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential roof replacements are completed in 1-2 days, depending on the size and complexity of your roof. Weather conditions and unforeseen issues can occasionally extend the timeline, but we keep you informed every step of the way.

How much does a roof replacement cost?

Every roof is different, which is why we provide free inspections and detailed estimates tailored to your specific needs. Factors like roof size, pitch, materials, and complexity all affect pricing. Contact us for an accurate, no-obligation estimate.
