Roofing systems

Commercial Metal Roofing Systems

Standing Seam Metal Roofing

Premium concealed-fastener roofing system delivering superior weather protection, clean aesthetics, and maximum longevity for commercial properties.

Key Features:

  • Concealed fasteners eliminate penetration points and leak risks
  • Vertical seam design allows thermal expansion and contraction
  • Available in multiple seam heights (1", 1.5", 2", 3") and panel widths (12", 16", 18", 24")
  • Steel, aluminum, zinc, and copper options
  • Factory-applied PVDF finishes with 25-40 year warranties

Benefits:

  • Watertight performance with raised interlocking seams
  • Exceptional wind resistance up to 140 mph
  • 50-70 years of reliable performance
  • Clean, modern appearance with no visible fasteners
  • Minimal maintenance over decades of service
  • Thermal movement accommodation prevents stress damage

Ideal for: Office buildings, retail centers, institutional facilities, industrial buildings, warehouses, religious facilities, sports facilities.

R-Panel Metal Roofing

Cost-effective exposed-fastener roofing system offering proven durability and reliable performance for commercial and industrial applications.

Key Features:

  • Exposed fastener system for economical installation
  • 1.25" high ribs spaced 12" on center
  • 36" panel coverage reduces seams
  • Available in 26-gauge and 29-gauge steel, plus aluminum
  • Wide range of factory-applied colors and finishes

Benefits:

  • Budget-friendly material and installation costs
  • Fast installation reduces project duration
  • Proven performance in demanding environments
  • Excellent structural strength for snow and equipment loads
  • Easy repairs and panel replacement
  • 30-50 years of reliable service
  • Versatile for roofs and walls

Ideal for: Warehouses, distribution centers, manufacturing facilities, agricultural buildings, storage facilities, automotive facilities, utility buildings, budget-conscious projects.

Structural Metal Panels

Heavy-duty metal roofing designed for large-span applications requiring maximum strength.

Key Features:

  • Self-supporting panels span long distances without purlins
  • Deep rib profiles (3" and 5" heights) provide superior structural strength
  • Reduces or eliminates secondary framing requirements
  • Engineered for high wind and snow load requirements

Benefits:

  • Reduces overall construction costs
  • Faster installation with fewer components
  • Excellent for retrofit applications
  • Long clear spans without interior supports

Ideal for: Large warehouses, distribution centers, manufacturing plants, aircraft hangars, sports facilities.

Comparison

Standing Seam vs. R-Panel

Standing Seam

  • Lifecycle cost: Higher
  • Lifespan: 50-70 years
  • Fasteners: Concealed
  • Maintenance: Minimal
  • Wind resistance: Up to 140 mph
  • Thermal movement: Accommodates expansion
  • Best for: High-visibility buildings

R-Panel

  • Lifecycle cost: Lower
  • Lifespan: 30-50 years
  • Fasteners: Exposed
  • Maintenance: Moderate (fastener checks)
  • Wind resistance: Up to 110 mph
  • Thermal movement: Limited movement
  • Best for: Industrial/warehouse applications

Choose Standing Seam for: Premium appearance, maximum longevity, minimal maintenance, high-visibility properties, coastal environments, buildings requiring superior weather protection.

Choose R-Panel for: Budget-conscious projects, industrial facilities, warehouses, agricultural buildings, functional applications where cost-effectiveness is priority.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does commercial metal roofing last?

Standing seam systems last 50-70 years. R-panel systems last 30-50 years. Steel systems average 40-50 years, while aluminum, zinc, and copper can exceed 70 years with minimal maintenance.

Is metal roofing more expensive than other commercial systems?

Initial costs are higher, but metal roofing's longevity, low maintenance, and energy savings result in lower lifecycle costs compared to TPO, EPDM, or built-up roofing.

What's the difference between standing seam and R-panel?

Standing seam uses concealed fasteners and raised seams for premium performance and longevity. R-panel uses exposed fasteners for economical installation. Standing seam lasts longer and requires less maintenance but costs more initially.

Can metal roofing be installed over existing roofs?

Yes, in many cases metal roofing can be installed over existing systems, reducing tear-off costs and waste. We assess structural capacity and building codes to determine feasibility.

How does metal roofing perform in high winds?

Properly installed standing seam roofing withstands wind speeds up to 140 mph. R-panel systems handle winds up to 110 mph. Both offer superior wind uplift resistance.

Does metal roofing require maintenance?

Standing seam requires minimal maintenance. R-panel requires periodic fastener checks and resealing every 5-10 years. We recommend bi-annual inspections for all metal roofing systems.

Will metal roofing reduce energy costs?

Yes. Reflective metal roofing reduces cooling costs by 10-25% by reflecting solar heat rather than absorbing it.

Is metal roofing noisy during rain?

Not with proper installation. Solid roof decking and building insulation effectively dampen sound. Most occupants notice no difference from other roofing types.

What warranties are available?

Metal roofing includes manufacturer warranties on materials (25-40 years on finishes, often lifetime on substrate) plus our workmanship warranty covering installation.
