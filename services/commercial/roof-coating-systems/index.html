NanoTech ICP™

Revolutionary NanoTech Insulative Ceramic Particle™ Technology

Nitro Roofing proudly offers NanoTech's groundbreaking Insulative Ceramic Particle™ technology, the most advanced thermal barrier system available for commercial roofing.

How ICP™ Technology Works

NanoTech's proprietary Insulative Ceramic Particles create an exceptional thermal barrier through three mechanisms:

Solar Reflection: Specialized ceramic particles reflect up to 90% of solar radiation before it penetrates your roof surface, preventing heat absorption.

Heat Dissipation: Advanced ceramic technology actively dissipates absorbed heat, preventing thermal transfer into your building.

Insulative Barrier: Microscopic ceramic particles create a continuous insulative layer that reduces thermal conductivity and stabilizes interior temperatures.

Performance Results

  • Surface temperature reduction: Up to 50°F
  • Interior temperature reduction: 10-20°F
  • Energy cost reduction: 20-40% on cooling expenses
  • Roof membrane stress reduction: Minimizes thermal cycling and UV degradation
  • Extended roof life: 10-20 additional years of performance

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do roof coatings last?

Quality roof coatings typically last 10-20 years depending on coating type, application thickness, and maintenance.

Can any roof be coated?

Most commercial roofs are excellent candidates. We assess roof condition, moisture content, and structural integrity to determine suitability.

How much can I save on energy costs?

Most properties see 20-40% reduction in cooling costs. We provide detailed savings projections.

Is coating better than replacement?

If your roof structure is sound and moisture-free, coating is often the smarter choice at 50-70% lower cost with minimal disruption.

What maintenance do coated roofs require?

Minimal. We recommend bi-annual inspections, debris removal, and addressing minor issues promptly.

What warranties are available?

Coating systems include manufacturer warranties on materials, typically 10-20 years, plus our workmanship warranty.
