Certifications

Industry Certifications & Awards

GAF Master Elite®

Nitro Roofing & Construction has earned GAF Master Elite® status — a certification awarded to only a small percentage of roofing contractors nationwide. To qualify, contractors must maintain proper licensing and insurance, demonstrate a proven reputation for quality workmanship, commit to ongoing professional training, and consistently deliver exceptional customer satisfaction.

Learn more about GAF Master Elite®

Ply Gem® Mastic®

As a trusted installer of Ply Gem and Mastic exterior products, Nitro Roofing & Construction aligns with manufacturers known for high-performance siding systems and contractor training standards. Participation in Ply Gem's professional contractor programs reflects a commitment to product expertise, ongoing education, and quality exterior installations built to perform for years to come.

GAF President's Club Residential Award Winner

The GAF President's Club Award is one of the highest honors in the roofing industry, reserved exclusively for top-performing GAF Master Elite® contractors. Recipients must demonstrate excellence in workmanship, reliability, customer service, and long-term performance while maintaining the strict standards required by GAF's elite certification program.

FORTIFIED™ Roof

Nitro Roofing & Construction is qualified to install FORTIFIED Roof™ systems designed to better withstand severe weather, high winds, and hail. Earning this designation requires specialized training, strict installation standards, and independent third-party verification to ensure roofs meet the resilience requirements established by the Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety (IBHS).

Learn more about FORTIFIED™ Roof

Metal Roofing Alliance (MRA)

Nitro Roofing & Construction is affiliated with the Metal Roofing Alliance (MRA), a leading organization dedicated to advancing quality residential metal roofing across North America. MRA contractors are recognized for their commitment to professional standards, industry best practices, ongoing education, and durable metal roofing solutions homeowners can trust.

Learn more about Metal Roofing Alliance (MRA)

DaVinci Masterpiece Contractor

Nitro Roofing & Construction was recognized in 2025 with the DaVinci® Masterpiece Contractor Project of the Year Award for excellence in craftsmanship, design execution, and attention to detail on a standout roofing project. This award honors contractors whose installations demonstrate exceptional visual impact, superior workmanship, and the ability to completely transform a home through premium roofing solutions.
